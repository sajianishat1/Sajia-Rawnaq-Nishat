LDLR mutations in young myocardial infarction Ongoing

Thesis title: Identification and Functional Characterization of LDLR Mutations in Young Myocardial Infarction Patients in Rajshahi Region

  • Collecting samples from patients at Rajshahi Medical College Hospital, then extracting DNA, running PCR and Sanger sequencing to find candidate variants.
  • Using protein structural modeling to assess how the variants affect the LDL receptor.
  • Building toward a region-specific mutation database to support early screening for premature cardiac disease in Bangladesh.
  • Funded by the National Science and Technology (NST) Fellowship, Ministry of Science and Technology, Bangladesh.

BSc project
University of Rajshahi

Multidrug-resistant Staphylococcus aureus and Streptococcus pneumoniae in pneumonia patients

Emergence of Multidrug-Resistant S. aureus and S. pneumoniae Among Pneumonia Patients in Rajshahi, Bangladesh: Implications for Empirical Treatment

  • Isolated bacteria from community-acquired pneumonia patients at Rajshahi Medical College Hospital, using Gram staining and catalase testing.
  • Tested susceptibility to 12 antibiotics by disk diffusion.
  • Confirmed species with DNA extraction and 16S rRNA PCR.
  • Found multidrug resistance in all isolates; tigecycline was the only antibiotic effective against every strain.
