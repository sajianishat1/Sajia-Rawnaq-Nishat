# Multidrug-Resistant *S. aureus* and *S. pneumoniae* in Pneumonia Patients, Rajshahi

> **Status:** completed BSc project, University of Rajshahi
> **Setting:** isolates from community-acquired pneumonia patients, Rajshahi Medical College Hospital

## Overview

**Project title:** Emergence of Multidrug-Resistant *Staphylococcus aureus* and *Streptococcus pneumoniae* Among Pneumonia Patients in Rajshahi, Bangladesh: Implications for Empirical Treatment

The project profiled antibiotic resistance in two major pneumonia-causing bacteria, to inform which antibiotics are reasonable choices when treatment must begin before lab results are available (empirical treatment).

## Methods

| Step | Technique |
|---|---|
| Isolation and phenotyping | Bacterial isolation, Gram staining, catalase testing |
| Resistance profiling | Disk diffusion susceptibility testing against 12 antibiotics |
| Species identification | DNA extraction and 16S rRNA PCR amplification |

## Key finding

Multidrug resistance was found among all of the isolates, and tigecycline was the only antibiotic found to be effective against all strains.

## Repository contents

- `docs/`: study summary and methods
- `data/`: de-identified susceptibility summary table (no patient identifiers)
- `analysis/`: scripts for summarizing and plotting resistance profiles
