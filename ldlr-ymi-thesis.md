# LDLR Mutations in Young Myocardial Infarction (Rajshahi Region)

> **Status:** ongoing MSc thesis, University of Rajshahi
> **Funding:** National Science and Technology (NST) Fellowship 2025–2026, Ministry of Science and Technology, Bangladesh
> **Lab:** Molecular Biology and Protein Science Laboratory, University of Rajshahi

## Overview

Young myocardial infarction (YMI) is a heart attack at an early age, and inherited variants in the *LDLR* gene (which encodes the LDL receptor) are a known genetic risk factor. This project looks for *LDLR* mutations in YMI patients in the Rajshahi region and studies how they may affect receptor function.

**Thesis title:** Identification and Functional Characterization of LDLR Mutations in Young Myocardial Infarction Patients in Rajshahi Region

## Aims

1. Identify candidate *LDLR* variants in YMI patients from the Rajshahi region.
2. Characterize how the variants may affect the LDL receptor, using computational and protein structural modeling.
3. Build a region-specific mutation database to support early screening for premature cardiac disease in Bangladesh.

## Methods

| Stage | Approach |
|---|---|
| Sample collection | Patient samples from Rajshahi Medical College Hospital |
| Wet lab | DNA extraction, PCR amplification, Sanger sequencing |
| Variant analysis | Mutation screening and candidate variant identification |
| Functional characterization | Computational / protein structural modeling of the impact of each variant |
| Output | Region-specific mutation database |
